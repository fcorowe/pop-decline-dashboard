About & cite

Newsham, N., & Rowe, F. (2023). Understanding trajectories of population decline across rural and urban Europe: A sequence analysis. Population, Space and Place, 29(3), e2630. First published online in 2022.

The study classifies annual population change for 696 declining areas from 2000 to 2018. Sequence analysis groups areas with similar patterns of annual change into seven trajectories.

Newsham, N., & Rowe, F. (2025). The demographic causes of European sub-national population declines. European Journal of Population, 41, Article 10.

The study uses demographic decomposition to estimate how fertility, mortality and net migration contributed to change in 732 declining areas. The authors group areas with similar component profiles into five signatures using multivariate functional principal component analysis and k-medoid clustering.

Boundaries are simplified for the map. Some names were matched by spelling similarity, and the Central Banat boundary was identified from its location. Signature assignments follow the demographic rates file. The 2011 population changes may partly reflect census revisions. Coastal measures are withheld pending confirmation of the study definition.
